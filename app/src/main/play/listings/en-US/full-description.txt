SmugView is a fast, beautifully designed viewer for public SmugMug photography sites. Type in any SmugMug nickname or custom domain and explore its folders, galleries, and photos with a fluid, gesture-driven interface built for browsing — not just viewing.

KEY FEATURES

• Explore any public site — enter any SmugMug nickname to browse its full folder and gallery structure, no account needed.
• Password-protected galleries — enter a gallery password once and SmugView remembers it securely for next time.
• Smart search — find folders, galleries, and photos across a site in one search, with instant local results plus live SmugMug search.
• Tag filtering — a swipeable tag sheet lets you include or exclude keywords to narrow huge galleries down to exactly what you want.
• Immersive full-screen viewer — swipe between photos with smooth paging, double-tap to favorite, and long-press for a quick-info overlay.
• Photo details — view camera settings, capture date, and keywords for any photo.
• Save & share — download full-resolution originals to your device or share a photo's SmugMug link directly.
• Offline collections — build your own custom collections and mark them for offline sync, so your favorites are viewable without a connection.
• Cast to the big screen — send galleries to Chromecast, Roku, and Fire TV devices right from your phone.

Whether you're catching up on a photographer's latest gallery, digging through a huge back catalog by tag, or just want a nicer way to browse SmugMug on your phone, SmugView is built to make it quick and enjoyable.

SmugView is an independent app built with the public SmugMug API. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by SmugMug, Inc.
