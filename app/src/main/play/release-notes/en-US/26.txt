New:
- The "new" dot now covers galleries inside password-protected folders, after a one-time scan of your whole site.
- The system Back button now works the way you expect on every tab: it steps back through folders, returns to Search, or closes an open collection.
- Where you were (folder, tab and last search) is restored if Android closes the app in the background.
- Folders you have not unlocked show a lock icon.

Fixed:
- Switching sites no longer mixes in folders or galleries from the previous site.
- Double-tapping a folder or gallery opens it once.
- A saved gallery password is no longer forgotten after a network error; it is only removed when you enter a wrong one.
- Unlocking a folder from Search or Home now opens the gallery you chose.
- Offline with nothing saved now shows a clear message instead of an endless spinner.
- Fixed several crashes and a hang when folders linked back to each other.
