# SmugView Support

SmugView is an independent viewer for public [SmugMug](https://www.smugmug.com) photography sites, for Android, iPhone and iPad. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by SmugMug, Inc.

## Getting started

1. Open SmugView and tap **Add a site**.
2. Type a SmugMug nickname or custom domain (for example `smugmugfilms`). No account is needed.
3. Browse folders and galleries, search, and tap a photo to see it full screen.

## Common questions

**A gallery asks for a password.** Enter the password once. SmugView keeps it in your device's secure storage (the Keychain on iPhone and iPad) so you don't have to enter it again. Passwords are sent only to SmugMug.

**Why can't I see some galleries?** SmugView shows what SmugMug makes public. Private galleries and folders locked with a password need that password.

**How do I keep photos for offline use?** Add photos to a collection, or choose **Keep offline** on a gallery. Offline downloads use Wi-Fi by default; the setting is in the app's offline settings.

**Can I save or share a photo?** Yes. Saving puts the full-size photo in your Photos library (iPhone and iPad) or your Pictures folder (Android). Sharing a link sends the SmugMug page; sharing a picture removes camera details and location first.

**How do I show photos on a TV?** Use the cast button in a gallery. On iPhone and iPad it finds Chromecast and other Google Cast devices (iOS asks for local network permission the first time) and works with AirPlay displays. Your phone and the TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network.

**The app says I'm offline, or that SmugMug is busy.** "Offline" means your device has no connection. If SmugMug is rate-limiting requests, wait a minute and try again.
