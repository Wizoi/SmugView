## Description
SmugView is a fast, beautifully designed viewer for public SmugMug photography sites. Type in any SmugMug nickname or custom domain and explore its folders, galleries, and photos with a fluid, gesture-driven interface built for browsing, not just viewing. No account needed.

KEY FEATURES

• Explore any public site: enter a SmugMug nickname to browse its full folder and gallery structure.
• Password-protected galleries: enter a gallery password once and SmugView remembers it securely in your device's Keychain.
• Smart search: find folders, galleries, and photos across a whole site in one search.
• Tag filtering: include or leave out keywords to narrow thousands of photos down to exactly what you want.
• Sorted by when the photo was taken: browse a photographer's work from the first shot to the latest, or the other way round.
• Immersive full-screen viewer: swipe between photos, and use your iPad or iPhone in either orientation.
• Photo details: camera, lens, exposure settings, capture date, and keywords for any photo.
• Save and share: save full-resolution originals to your Photos library, or share a photo's link, a QR code, or the picture itself at Mobile or Original size, and send it with AirDrop. Camera details and location are removed from shared JPEGs.
• Collections and offline: build your own collections, and keep whole galleries on your device to view without a connection.
• New-gallery alerts: follow a site and get a notification when it publishes a new gallery. Blue dots show what changed since you last looked, and one tap clears them.
• Show it on the big screen: send galleries to a Chromecast or other Google Cast device, or to an AirPlay display.

Whether you are catching up on a photographer's latest gallery, digging through a huge back catalog by tag, or just want a nicer way to browse SmugMug on your iPhone or iPad, SmugView is built to make it quick and enjoyable. A short "How to use SmugView" guide is one tap away inside the app.

SmugView is an independent app built with the public SmugMug API. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by SmugMug, Inc.
